I've been listening to Kiefer since about sophomore year of college, and I just so happened to be going on a long bike ride the day this album dropped.
I listened to it on my ride and several times since.
It's just a cozy, good-times album, featuring lots of talented indie jazz artists such as Nate Smith.
If I had any gripe at all, it would be that one of the things I like most about Kiefer's catalogue was that there aren't many vocal tracks, which I enjoy for listening while working. 
That said, the vocal tunes on this album are quite well done, particularly **Get to the Moon**.
